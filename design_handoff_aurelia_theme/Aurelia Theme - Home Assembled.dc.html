Before you buy

The things people write to us about most. If yours isn't here, someone at the bench answers the email.

Write to us
I don't know my ring size.−

Order the free brass sizer — it arrives in a letter, not a parcel. Resizing is free for life, up to three sizes either way, so a near-miss is not a problem.

How long until it arrives?+

Three days for pieces in stock, ten if engraved, about twenty-one for made-to-order and archive commissions.

Can I return an engraved piece?+

Yes — thirty days, engraved or not. The only exception is archive pieces, which are final sale because they are cast individually.

Is the gold really recycled?+

All of it, and we keep the refinery invoice for every batch. Ask and we will send you the one your piece came from.
