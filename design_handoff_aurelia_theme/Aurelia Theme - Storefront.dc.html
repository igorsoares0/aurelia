9eFAQ — native disclosures, one open by default, questions in the customer's words

Before you buy

The six things people write to us about most. If yours isn't here, someone at the bench answers the email.

Write to us
I don't know my ring size.−

Order the free brass sizer — it arrives in a letter, not a parcel. And resizing is free for life, up to three sizes either way, so a near-miss is not a problem.

How long until it arrives?+

Three days for pieces in stock, ten if engraved, about twenty-one for made-to-order and archive commissions. Shipping is insured and included, worldwide.

Can I return an engraved piece?+

Yes — thirty days, engraved or not. The only exception is archive pieces, which are final sale because they are cast individually.

Is the gold really recycled?+

All of it, and we keep the refinery invoice for every batch. Ask and we will send you the one your piece came from.

Will you remake something I already own?+

Often. Bring an heirloom and we melt, trace and rebuild it as something worn daily. It starts with a photograph and a conversation.
