Same sections, same markup, same accessibility contract. What differs is only what a preset can carry: the two font_picker values, the colour settings, and a handful of layout flags. Each card shows the identical slice of the storefront — header, hero, statement, product grid, dark band — plus the exact settings that produce it. Every accent below clears 4.5:1 on its own ground.
