14aControls — every state of every interactive element. Copy from here, not from a screen.

Controls

Buttons — 52px primary, 48px secondary, 44px minimum. Labels never wrap: nowrap + padding, never a fixed width

primary hover focus disabled
secondary on dark quiet / text

Never more than one filled button in a view. Uppercase labels always carry .16–.18em tracking — without it the weight reads as shouting. Disabled is a real disabled attribute, not a faded div.

Fields

That address is missing its domain.

A placeholder is an example, never a label. Error text sits under the field, tied by aria-describedby, and says what to do — not "invalid input".

The one rule that breaks silently: these pickers hide the real input, so the focus ring must be drawn on the visible child — label:focus-within > span. Lose that rule and the primary buy control becomes invisible to keyboard users. Unavailable is dashed and explained in text; colour alone never carries meaning.

Hairlines — the whole visual language

.10 — between rows in a list

.14 — between sections, and header/footer edges

.18 — under a heading that labels a group; box outlines

.30 — field and control borders

1.5px ink — selected, and table header rules

Rules do the work that shadows and cards do elsewhere. One shadow exists in the whole theme — under a sheet or drawer that floats above the page. No rounded corners anywhere except the metal swatch dot.

14cPhotography and copy — the two things the merchant executes without us

Art direction & voice

Four kinds of photograph, and nothing else

  • Worn

    3:4. A body, cropped. Daylight, one source. Hero and lookbook only.

  • Object

    3:4. Stone or paper ground in the off-white family. The piece fills 60% of frame.

  • Macro

    1:1 or 3:4. Close enough to see tool marks. Proves the making claim.

  • Bench

    4:5 or wide. Hands and tools, warm light. Only in dark sections.

Never: pure white or black backgrounds, cut-outs floating on nothing, ring boxes, rose petals, gift ribbon, sparkle overlays, or two pieces competing in one frame. Every photograph is either a body, an object on a real surface, a detail, or the workshop. Every image on the site has a caption or an alt that says what it is — "Fig. 01 — The Signet, 18k recycled gold", not "jewellery".

Voice

Write this

  • Ready to ship in 3 days
  • Twelve pieces a year. Each one kept forever.
  • Size 18 is unavailable in brushed yellow
  • Resizing is free for life, up to three sizes
  • Shop the chapter

Not this

  • Fast shipping!!
  • Timeless elegance for the modern woman
  • Out of stock
  • Lifetime warranty*
  • SHOP NOW
Specific
Three days, 9.4g, size 14, by Túlio. Numbers instead of adjectives.
Plain
Sentence case. One exclamation mark in the whole store is one too many.
Honest
Say the constraint out loud: it takes twenty-one days, archive pieces are final sale.
Unhurried
No countdowns, no "only 2 left" pressure, no fake scarcity. Scarcity here is real; state it flatly.
Labels
Uppercase eyebrows are nouns — Chapter One, Materials, House services. Never verbs.

Motion

Scroll reveal
Opacity 0→1 with a 20px rise, 900ms, cubic-bezier(.2,.7,.2,1). Once per element, never on replay.
Hover
Colour and border only, 150–200ms. Nothing scales, lifts, or rotates.
Sheets
Slide from the edge they belong to, 260ms ease-out. Focus moves in; Escape closes.
Never
Parallax, carousel autoplay, counting numbers, letter-by-letter type-in, video that starts itself.
Always
All of it off under prefers-reduced-motion, and forced visible before print or when the tab hides.
